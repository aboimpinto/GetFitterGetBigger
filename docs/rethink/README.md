# GetFitterGetBigger: product rethink

Discussion draft, 6 September 2026. Working name: **Repward**. The name, product scope, and design are proposals, not decisions or implemented features.

The proposed promise is: **Bring your training. See your progress.**

Keep a durable record of what someone actually did, relate that work to their goals, explain the measurements, and make small competitions motivating and understandable. Training can come from the user, a coach, a template, or an AI assistant.

- [Code and recovery assessment](code-assessment.md): what exists, what is partial, and what needs rebuilding.
- [Product, interactions, and naming](product-direction.md): positioning, roles, measurements, competition, screens, and a first release.
- [Design language](design-language.md): charcoal backgrounds and red accents, following Paulo's direction on 7 September 2026.
- [Current visual concept](design-concept-dark-red.png): a dark admin measurement editor and client progress screen, using fictional demonstration data.

The assessment covers `master` at `f56aa61c5fedd69fe525e035b37ab153b838d034` (latest commit dated 11 September 2025). It is a source inspection, not a successful build, runtime acceptance test, database restoration, or exhaustive security audit. No application features have been changed, published, or deployed.

The first-release proposal assumes people tracking their own gym progress, with optional friends. Coaches and clubs remain part of the product structure; the audience choice is open for discussion.

The original `design-concept.png` is retained as a superseded exploration. Its light/teal palette is no longer the design direction. The dark concept is an image-generation edit; [generation brief](design-concept-dark-red-prompt.md). Neither image is an implemented application screen.
